# 0011. The repository is public under the MIT license

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

Portolab may help lecturers beyond its first course. GitHub's free plan enforces branch rules only on public repositories.

## Decision

The repository is public from its first commit, under the MIT license.

## Alternatives

- **AGPL.** Requires anyone who runs a modified version as a service to publish their changes.
- **Private until the first release.** Branch rules would go unenforced until then.

## Consequences

- Anyone may reuse the code, including commercially.
- Nothing personal and no secret may ever enter the repository.
