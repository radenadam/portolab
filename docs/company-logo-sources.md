# Company logo sources

These public addresses are the sources of the tiles of the approved prototype. They cover its stocks only. The tiles were made again on a transparent ground on 19 November 2026, from the original files below, which were downloaded that day. The owner chooses and supplies files under [the deployment guide](deploy.md#optional-company-logos). The logos belong to their companies. The repository keeps these addresses without downloading or redistributing the images.
