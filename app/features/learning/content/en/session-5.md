# Instruments of the capital market

## summary

Distinguish each instrument's rights, obligations, and sources of risk.

## key-idea

Different instruments give claims on different cash flows, so their risks differ too.

## body

A stock is ownership in a company. Holders may receive dividends if declared and gain or lose from price changes. Neither dividends nor price gains are guaranteed.

A bond is debt with payment terms. A mutual fund pools investors' money in a portfolio managed by an investment manager. Derivatives derive value from another asset or reference and can create additional obligations.

Before comparing returns, ask who must pay, when you can access the money, and what could cause a loss. Portolab's trading practice uses stocks, while the other instruments are covered in the material.
