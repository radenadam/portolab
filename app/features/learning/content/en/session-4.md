# The capital market

## summary

See how funding reaches companies and how securities are traded.

## key-idea

The primary market raises funds for issuers, while the secondary market lets investors trade securities already issued.

## body

In Indonesia, IDX operates exchange trading. KPEI handles clearing and guarantees exchange-trade settlement, while KSEI provides securities custody and settlement. OJK regulates and supervises capital-market activities.

Investors send orders through securities firms. Prices form from buyers' and sellers' orders, so the last price is not a promise about the next trade price.

An index summarises the prices of a group of stocks. The IHSG is the market benchmark in Portolab's measures. LQ45 has its own selection criteria, so membership is not a recommendation to buy.
