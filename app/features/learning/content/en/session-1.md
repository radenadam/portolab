# Basic concepts of portfolio management

## summary

Start with a goal, then choose the risk you can bear.

## key-idea

A portfolio is a whole, so judge each asset choice by its effect on the whole.

## body

Before choosing assets, set a goal, a time horizon, cash needs, and your capacity to bear losses. Money you need soon has different constraints from money for a long-term goal.

Choose an allocation that fits, then monitor whether its weights still suit your goal. Price changes can change the weights even when you make no trades.

A high return alone does not explain the quality of a decision. Record the risk you took and why you chose it. Use later results to reconsider that reason.
