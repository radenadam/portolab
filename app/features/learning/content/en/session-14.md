# Bonds

## summary

A bond's payment promise still carries price risk and default risk.

## key-idea

A bond's value comes from its promised cash flows and the yield the market requires.

## body

A bond has terms for repayment of principal and, if any, coupons. Coupons can be fixed or floating, and some bonds have no coupons. Face value is not the price always paid in the market.

For a fixed-coupon bond, a rise in the yield the market requires lowers the present value of its fixed payments. Its price therefore falls if other factors stay the same. Duration measures price sensitivity to yield changes.

The coupon differs from yield to maturity. Purchase price and repayment of principal also matter. Consider the issuer's ability to pay, inflation, liquidity, and provisions for repayment before maturity.
