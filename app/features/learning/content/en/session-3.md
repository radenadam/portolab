# The time value of money

## summary

Compare cash flows at the same point in time.

## key-idea

The value of money depends on when you receive it and the relevant rate of return.

## body

Money available now can be invested. With a positive rate of return, the same amount received in the future is worth less today.

Compounding carries a present value into the future. Discounting brings a future cash flow back to a present value. Match the rate and the number of periods to the timing of the cash flows.

The discount rate reflects comparable investment opportunities and cash-flow risk. The BI-Rate is a policy rate, so it is not automatically the right discount rate for every investment.
