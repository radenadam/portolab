# Mutual funds

## summary

A fund unit's value follows the portfolio managed for its unitholders.

## key-idea

Buying units gives you a share of a pooled portfolio rather than a guaranteed return.

## body

An investment manager manages the money under the investment policy, and a custodian bank holds the assets. Net asset value is asset value after liabilities. Value per unit relates it to the number of outstanding units.

Fund categories distinguish objectives and assets, such as money market, fixed income, equity, and balanced funds. The name fixed income does not mean a unit's value stays fixed.

Check the investment policy, costs, risks, and redemption terms in the prospectus. A fund concentrated in one sector need not provide broad diversification.
