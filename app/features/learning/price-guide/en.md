# How to read a stock price

## key-idea

A price row shows the stock code, price per share, change, and quote time.

## code

The stock code identifies a company. Its name sits below the code so you can distinguish companies with similar codes.

## price

The last price is the price per share in the latest quote Portolab received. It does not include the purchase fee.

## change

The change compares the last price with the close on the trading day before the quoted day. A plus sign and upward arrow show a rise, while a minus sign and downward arrow show a fall.

## time

The quote time tells you when the price occurred in the market, rather than when you opened the page. After trading ends, the last quote can remain visible until the next update. A price that could not be updated carries a notice beside it.
